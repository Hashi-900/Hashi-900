## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an economist with double master’s degrees, deeply fascinated by the intersection of **economics and machine learning**. Based in Germany, my professional interests and expertise lie in data analytics, machine learning engineering, and business intelligence.

My background in economics equips me with a perspective that many data scientists often lack: the ability to rigorously frame business problems, understand the underlying drivers of consumer decision-making, and approach challenges through the lens of **causal inference** rather than mere prediction. 

I design and develop machine learning pipelines, Quarto dashboards, and advanced statistical models that empower organisations to make informed, evidence-driven decisions, with a particular emphasis on demand estimation, causal inference, and counterfactual analysis for pricing strategy optimisation.

> **My Economics Edge:**
> - I think in terms of **causal inference**, not just correlation.
> - I model **uncertainty** and communicate it clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
> - I connect analytical output directly to **business or policy decisions**.
